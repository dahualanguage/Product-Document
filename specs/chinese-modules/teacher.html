Teacher — Content Creation

How a teacher builds source content and authors the four practice types, then assigns them to classes.

Chinese Modules Role: CLASS_TEACHER Source: dahua-dash Updated: Jun 2026
The chain. Projects + Question Banks feed → Practice authoring (4 types) → Assignment to classes. The Overview shows where this sits; the Student chapter covers what happens after assigning.

1 Projects

Content-rich source units that practices are built from. /dashboard/projects

  • Content types: Article, Story, Announcement, Script, Speech, Conversation, Debate Script
  • Metadata: target language (Simplified / Traditional), TOCFL/HSK level (1–7), teaching points (words, verbs, nouns, adjectives, grammar, phrases, measure words)
  • AI: generate content and (optionally) analyse teaching points

Creation flow

Setup ─▶ Generate content ─▶ Confirm (name, language, (AI or manual) (review & export) level, points)

Sub-pages: list · create · detail · slide preview · handout export

2 Question Banks

Reusable question libraries that feed Multiple Choice practices. /dashboard/questions-bank paid-feature gated

  • Question types: True/False, Multiple Choice
  • Categories: Comprehension, Pinyin, Meaning, Fill-in-blank
  • MC variants: Chinese→English, Image→Chinese, Image→Pinyin, English→Chinese, English→Pinyin, Audio→English, Audio→Chinese
  • Standalone or linked to a project; carries TOCFL/HSK level
Project (optional) ─▶ Generate wizard ─▶ View / export (add manually or AI)

3 Practice authoring

One unified 3–4 step stepper authors all four practice types. /dashboard/practice

TypeSource (Step 1)Key settingsStudent does
Q&AProject or manual textAudio, playback speed, pinyin, translationSpeaks an answer (record)
MirroringProject or manual textAudio mode, playback speedRepeats native audio (record)
VocabularyVocab from project / custom wordsSub-type: Mirroring · Mixed · Multiple Choice + pinyin/translationListen & speak / quiz
Multiple ChoiceQuestion bankEdit questions, optional exam timerSelects answers (click)
Vocabulary has three sub-types chosen at authoring time: Mirroring (listen & speak words), Mixed (combined exercises), Multiple Choice (word→meaning).

4 Assignment

The final step of authoring assigns the practice to classes/students. assign-practice.js

  • Assign to: individual classes or all classes
  • Type: Mandatory (hard due date) or Optional (expiry date)
  • Dates: due/expiry per class or global; optional start date controls availability
  • After assigning: teacher views submissions & attempts per assignment
Assignment is the hand-off point — see the Student — Practice chapter for what the learner sees next.
