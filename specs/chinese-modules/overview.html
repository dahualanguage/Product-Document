Chinese Modules — Overview

What the Chinese language-learning product is, who uses it, and the features at a glance. Teacher and Student details have their own chapters.

Chinese Modules Status: Current (as-built) Source: dahua-dash Updated: Jun 2026

1 What it is

A Chinese-language practice platform. Teachers create content and practices, then assign them to classes; students complete the assigned practices and submit them back for review. The assignment is the bridge between the two sides.

Teacher Student ───────────────────────── ───────────────────────── Projects ─┐ Question Banks ─┼─▶ Practice ─▶ Assignment ─▶ My Assignments ─▶ Practice session ─▶ Submit │ (Q&A / Mirroring / (Mandatory / (record / answer) to teacher │ Vocabulary / MC) Optional) └────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Scope. This covers the non-ESL Chinese product only. ESL is documented separately under 3. ESL Modules.

Where it lives — org structure

School ─▶ Program ─▶ Class ─▶ { Teachers · Students }

Teachers and students belong to a class; classes sit under a program; programs under a school. Practices are assigned at the class level.

4 Core concepts

4.1 · Practice vs Assignment

  • Practice — the authored unit (content + settings) for one of the 4 types.
  • Assignment — an instance of a practice given to a class with due date + submission tracking.

4.2 · Status flow

Not started ─▶ In progress ─▶ Ready to submit ─▶ Submitted / Completed (progress 0) (progress > 0) (all attempts done, (sent to teacher — not sent yet) "completed" = submitted)

4.3 · Mandatory vs Optional

MandatoryOptional
DeadlineHard due date; overdue if missedExpiry date; submit anytime before it
Unfinished after deadlineMarked overdue, stays visibleDisappears from the list
FlagisOptional = falseisOptional = true

4.4 · Scoring

  • Mirroring / Vocabulary / MC: automatic % score; Q&A: usually teacher-graded
  • Best score tracked across attempts; surfaces on cards, history and leaderboard
  • Word Game: earns Stars (1 per exercise, up to 5 per word); a word is Learned at 1–4 stars, Mastered at 5. No hearts/coins in the game itself.
  • Account stats: the sidebar ⭐ / ❤️ are account-level totalPoints / incentivePoints (backend), separate from the Word Game.
