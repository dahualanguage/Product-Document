Good morning class. Today we're going to learn about photosynthesis and how plants make their food.

First, let's talk about chlorophyll. It's the green pigment found in the leaves of plants.

Chlorophyll absorbs sunlight and uses it to convert carbon dioxide and water into glucose.

This process also produces oxygen, which is released into the atmosphere. That's what we breathe!

Now, the equation for photosynthesis is very important. Let me write it on the board for you.

Six molecules of carbon dioxide plus six molecules of water produces glucose and six molecules of oxygen.

The stomata on the surface of the leaf allow carbon dioxide to enter the plant.

Plants also need minerals from the soil. The roots absorb water and nutrients through the ground.

Without sunlight, photosynthesis cannot occur. That's why plants grow toward light sources.
