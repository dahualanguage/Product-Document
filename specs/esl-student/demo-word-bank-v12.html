hypothesis 假說
Translate
noun

A proposed explanation made on the basis of limited evidence as a starting point for further investigation.

基於有限證據所提出的解釋，作為進一步調查的起點。

Example
We need to test our hypothesis before drawing any conclusions.

在得出任何結論之前，我們需要先驗證我們的假說。
